Overnight Oats

Description

A simple, creamy breakfast made by soaking oats overnight with milk, yogurt, and your favorite toppings.

Ingredients

  • 1/2 cup rolled oats
  • 1/2 cup milk
  • 1/4 cup Greek yogurt
  • 1 tbsp honey or maple syrup
  • 1/2 cup fresh berries
  • 1 tbsp chopped nuts

Steps

  1. Add the oats, milk, yogurt, and honey to a jar.
  2. Stir until well combined.
  3. Cover the jar and refrigerate overnight.
  4. Stir the oats in the morning.
  5. Top with berries and chopped nuts.
  6. Serve cold and enjoy.
