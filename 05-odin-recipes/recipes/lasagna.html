Lasagna

Description

A classic, comforting lasagna made with layers of pasta, rich meat sauce, creamy ricotta, and melted mozzarella.

Ingredients

  • 9 lasagna noodles
  • 1 lb ground beef
  • 1 jar (24 oz) tomato pasta sauce
  • 15 oz ricotta cheese
  • 2 cups shredded mozzarella cheese
  • 1/2 cup grated Parmesan cheese
  • 1 egg

Steps

  1. Preheat the oven to 375°F (190°C).
  2. Cook the lasagna noodles according to the package directions. Drain and set aside.
  3. Brown the ground beef in a large pan. Drain excess fat and stir in the pasta sauce.
  4. In a bowl, mix the ricotta, egg, Italian seasoning, salt, and pepper.
  5. Spread a thin layer of meat sauce in a baking dish.
  6. Add a layer of noodles, ricotta mixture, meat sauce, and mozzarella. Repeat until the ingredients are used.
  7. Sprinkle Parmesan and the remaining mozzarella over the top.
  8. Cover with foil and bake for 25 minutes. Remove the foil and bake for another 15 minutes.
  9. Let the lasagna rest for 10 minutes before serving.
